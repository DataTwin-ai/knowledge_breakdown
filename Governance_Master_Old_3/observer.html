Where it comes from
The papers we get (invoice, registration, contract…). What no paper has, we ask the user.
Fact
One piece of data (“what was sold”). Fetched once, reused by every law. L1 is checked first, LAST at the end.
Atom
One yes / no question a law asks. Python answers simple ones; a pink edge = an AI judge reads text.
Group
Joins answers: ALL must be yes, or ANY one is enough.
Law expression
The law's full rule, built from atoms and groups. Orange = overrides another law.
Decision
What the law concludes. It can answer another law's check (dashed line back).
Self reference
The law points to another part of the same Act; that part's decisions answer its check.
Cross reference
The law points to a different document, inserted from outside (IGST Act…).
